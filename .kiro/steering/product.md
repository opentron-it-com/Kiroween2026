# Ghostbook product direction

Ghostbook is a new, distinct Resurrection-category experience built during the Kiroween 2026 submission period. It revives the personal digital organizer: users bring supported exported files into a haunted PDA-inspired workspace and use OpenTron's retrieval-backed assistant to rediscover information.

Optimize for one trustworthy demo path: import a synthetic archive, ask a concrete question, inspect the supporting source, and understand any failure. The spooky visual treatment must support the product rather than obscure content or controls.

Do not represent the existing OpenTron product, an old project, or a theme-only change as the new entry. Do not claim support for an archive format, local-only inference, or grounded citations until verified end to end. Keep private user data out of sample assets, analytics, screenshots, and logs.
